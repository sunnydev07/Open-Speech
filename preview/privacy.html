Privacy Policy — Fluency Coach

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Fluency Coach ("the app") is an AI-driven English speaking coach. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Microphone recordings

The app records your voice only while a practice session is actively running, after you tap the record button. Recordings are used to transcribe your speech and generate fluency, pronunciation, and pacing feedback. The app never records in the background.

AI processing (Google Gemini)

Speech audio and transcripts are sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis. Processing is subject to Google's API data policies. The app developer does not operate a separate server that stores your recordings.

On-device data

  • Practice stats (streaks, daily goal progress, average pacing) are stored on your device.
  • No account or sign-in is required. The app does not collect your name, email, or contacts.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell personal data. Audio and transcripts are shared only with the AI provider (Google Gemini) to the extent needed to produce your feedback, and as required by law.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will advise on removing local data.

Your choices

  • Deny microphone permission to prevent all recording (practice sessions will not function).
  • Uninstall the app to delete all on-device practice data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/sunnydev07/Open-Speech/issues.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
